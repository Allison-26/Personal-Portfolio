Soles Show Souls

For this project, I photographed the shoes of fellow college students as an alternative to traditional portraiture, capturing identity in a subtle yet intimate way. I chose the title Soles Show Souls because shoes, the soles we walk in, reveal a lot about who a person is. They reflect personality, style, and individuality, offering a glimpse into a person’s soul. Utilizing a film camera and developing the images myself in the darkroom allowed me to engage with every part of the process, from taking the shot to seeing the final print develop. I really enjoyed this project and the way I was able to highlight personal expression. I look forward to continuing this project in the future and experimenting with different mediums and demographics to further examine identity and the ways we express ourselves.
