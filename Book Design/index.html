Book Design

Anatomy

This collaborative book project reinterprets Aesop’s Fables through the metaphor of human anatomy, using the body as a system to organize interconnected stories. Working within a shared grid, typographic system, and color structure, I designed a series of individual spreads that pair each fable with a specific piece of human anatomy. I photographed each anatomical form from an anatomy textbook and used Photoshop to color grade the images to match the palette assigned to each body system to create visual harmony across each spread. Using InDesign I incorporated image, text, and negative space to establish a visual hierarchy, allowing each spread to function independently within a unified sequence.

In Cold Blood

For this project, I was asked to create an alternative book cover featuring abstract and illustrative elements. Using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, I worked with typography, the pen tool, line tool, gradient tool, shape tool, eraser tool, opacity, and adjustments to hue and saturation. To reflect the eerie tension of the true murder case, I chose black and red as dominant colors. The cover features an illustration of the house where the murders occurred, with a red-highlighted section to emphasize the real Clutter family home. I incorporated images of the victims and the convicted criminals to add authenticity. I chose a typewriter font to capture the investigative tone in the story. While In Cold Blood is based on true events, it fictionalized some aspects for dramatic effect. This choice reflects the dangers of assumption and how perception shapes reality. It shows how a narrative can be distorted by incomplete information which can lead to a twisted understanding of the truth.
