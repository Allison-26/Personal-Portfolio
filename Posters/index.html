Ag as AI

For this poster I explored how generative AI disrupts originality. Using Adobe Illustrator, Lightroom, and Photoshop, I employed tools like opacity, the pen tool, typography, and masking to make my image. I chose to take my own self-portrait to reflect my personal struggle with the impact of AI. This design represents how AI overwhelms and dominates the creative process and slowly begins taking over. I placed the words “Substitution,” “Contamination,” and “Invasion” over the glitching parts of the image to symbolize the intrusiveness of AI. The futuristic font is difficult to read which mirrors how AI is confusing and often illegible. I placed a handwritten text on my skin to emphasize how true originality comes from humans. AI’s prominence in our society and the artistic world weakens our uniqueness, and intrudes on creativity. This piece reflects the tension between human craftsmanship and the growing influence of artificial intelligence in the creative process.

Something Needs to Change

The goal of this project was to create a three-poster campaign addressing a contemporary social issue I find important and inspiring. I chose bullying, a topic that I find deeply significant. Using Adobe Illustrator, I employed the pen tool, experimented with opacity, and focused on typography to convey a powerful message. I chose symbolic colors to represent different types of bullying. Blue represents cyberbullying, which is often linked to screens. Red symbolizes physical bullying, conveying a sense of violence and agression. Green stands for social bullying and represents isolation and envy which are emotions often felt by those who are excluded and manipulated within social groups. I wanted each poster to be distinct yet cohesive, ensuring they worked together as a campaign while still being able to stand alone in their impact. Through this project, I aimed to raise awareness about the harsh reality of bullying and encourage individuals to stop the mistreatment of others.
